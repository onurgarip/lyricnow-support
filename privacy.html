Privacy Policy

LyricNow · Last updated 30 September 2026

Audio is never recorded or saved. There are no accounts. LyricNow is free and shows a banner ad served by Google AdMob, which you can remove with a one-time purchase. Anonymous usage statistics help improve the app; they never include the songs you listen to.

Microphone

LyricNow uses the microphone for one purpose: recognizing music playing near your device. Audio is processed live to identify the song and is never written to storage, never transmitted to us, and never shared with anyone else.

The microphone is active only while the app is open and listening. It stops when you leave the app or tap the microphone button to pause. You can revoke microphone access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.

Song recognition

Song identification uses Apple's ShazamKit, which is built into iOS. A short, non-reversible acoustic fingerprint of the audio is sent to Apple's music recognition service to determine what is playing. This fingerprint cannot be converted back into audio and does not contain speech or recordings. Apple's handling of this data is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

Lyrics

Once a song is identified, LyricNow requests its lyrics from LRCLIB (lrclib.net), a free public lyrics database. Only the song title and artist name are sent. No identifier, device information, or personal data is included in the request.

Advertising

LyricNow shows a banner ad served by Google AdMob. To choose and measure ads, Google collects device and usage data such as an advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interactions. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • Tracking permission. iOS asks whether LyricNow may track you across apps. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalized using it. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland. Google's consent form asks for your choices before any ad is requested. You can change them later from “Remove ads” → “Privacy choices”.
  • Removing ads. After the Remove Ads purchase, no more ads are requested, and from the next launch Google's ad SDK is not started at all.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is processed by Apple. LyricNow uses RevenueCat to verify it and to restore it on your other devices; RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt and a random app-generated identifier, never your name, email or payment details. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy. RevenueCat also receives ad-revenue figures for the banner, linked to the same random identifier.

Usage statistics

LyricNow sends anonymous usage events to PostHog: for example that a song was identified, whether lyrics were found, or that listening was paused. Events are tied to a random identifier created on install — not to your name, email, Apple Account or advertising identifier — and never include song titles, artists, or audio. Device model, iOS version, app version and approximate location derived from your IP address are included. See the PostHog Privacy Policy.

Data we collect

Only the anonymous usage statistics and purchase status described above. The developer never receives audio, song history, contact details or your advertising identifier. We do not sell data.

Children

LyricNow is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes
